Principles

How I approach technical systems.

Automate judgment last.

Automate repeatable work first. Preserve human authority where uncertainty and consequence are high.

Observability is part of correctness.

A system that cannot explain what happened is difficult to operate, evaluate, or trust.

Architecture should survive tools.

Models, vendors, and frameworks change faster than the engineering principles connecting them.

Evidence over demos.

A system is not reliable because it worked once. Claims should be earned through repeatable evaluation.
